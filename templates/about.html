💡 About Error Level Analysis (ELA)

ELA works by re-saving the image at a known quality level and comparing it to the original. Areas with different compression levels will show up as brighter spots, potentially indicating manipulation.

Key Points:
  • Authentic images have consistent error levels
  • Manipulated areas show different compression artifacts
  • ELA highlights these inconsistencies for analysis
  • Deep learning model classifies these patterns

⚠️ Limitations & Disclaimer

Important: This tool provides probabilistic analysis, not definitive proof. Results should be interpreted as indicators rather than absolute truth.

Factors affecting accuracy include:

  • Image quality and compression level
  • Sophistication of manipulation techniques
  • Training data limitations
  • Image format and metadata preservation

Always use multiple verification methods for critical decisions.
