How It Works

📤

Upload

Upload your image or video file for analysis

🔍

Analyze

System first performs Error Level Analysis (ELA) on images or video frames to highlight compression artifacts caused by manipulation. The processed ELA images are fed into a CNN model, which learns to distinguish real vs fake patterns from these artifacts. For videos, multiple frames are sampled, converted to ELA, analyzed by the CNN, and the average confidence determines whether the video is REAL or FAKE.

📊

Get Results

After converting the input image or sampled video frames into ELA images, the CNN model predicts a probability score indicating how likely each frame is fake. For videos, all frame scores are averaged to get a final confidence. If this confidence crosses the threshold (e.g., 0.5 or 0.8), the system labels the input as FAKE, otherwise REAL.

About DeepScan

System uses ELA (Error Level Analysis) to highlight pixel-level compression artifacts, which are strong indicators of manipulation.These ELA-enhanced images are passed through a CNN-based classifier that learns the visual differences between real and fake content.For videos, the model processes sampled frames and averages their predictions, and produces a final confidence score identifying whether the video is real or fake.
